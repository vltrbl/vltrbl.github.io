Scope of practice

Not every kind of struggle needs the same kind of help. Here's where I can actually be useful — and where you deserve someone with different training.

What I work with

  • Anxiety and persistent worry
  • Sleep difficulties
  • Low mood and depressive episodes (non-clinical support)
  • Relationship and interpersonal difficulties
  • Stress and life transitions

What I don't work with

These need medical or clinical care I'm not licensed to provide — nothing personal, just not my lane. If any of the following applies to you, please reach out to a licensed clinician, psychiatrist, or one of the resources below instead of booking with me:

  • Psychotic disorders (e.g. schizophrenia)
  • Eating disorders (e.g. anorexia, bulimia)
  • Active suicidal crisis or self-harm requiring emergency care
  • Substance dependency requiring medical detox
  • Court-mandated therapy

If you're in immediate danger or crisis, please contact one of these instead of waiting for a session:

Germany — emergency: 112. TelefonSeelsorge (free, 24/7): 0800 111 0 111.

Ukraine — medical emergency: 103. Ministry of Health mental health support line (24/7): 0 800 505 501. National Psychological Association hotline (10:00–20:00 daily): 0 800 100 102.

Wherever you are, local emergency services or a crisis line can respond faster than a scheduled session.

Not sure where you fit? Just ask — I'd rather point you in the right direction than leave you guessing.
