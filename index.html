Starting over somewhere new brings its own weight — a different language, a different rhythm, family and friends now a video call away instead of a five-minute walk. That's often on top of whatever brought you here in the first place. It's a lot to carry alone.

I offer a space to talk it through in Ukrainian, Russian, or English, with someone who understands both the psychology and the specific experience of rebuilding a life abroad.

What I help with

Anxiety & worry

Racing thoughts, constant tension, or a sense of unease that won't switch off.

Sleep difficulties

Trouble falling asleep, staying asleep, or waking up rested.

Low mood

Periods of heaviness, low energy, or losing interest in things that used to matter. Non-clinical support.

Adjusting to a new country

Culture shock, isolation, and the practical and emotional weight of starting over.

Relationship difficulties

Conflict, distance, or disconnect with partners, family, or friends.

Stress & life transitions

Big changes — a move, a new job, a shift in family life — and the stress that comes with them.

Who I am

I'm a psychology graduate (B.Sc., Jacobs University Bremen) with several years of hands-on experience in crisis counselling — including supporting Ukrainian refugees directly, and working with veterans adjusting to civilian life. I offer consultation, not clinical treatment: practical, grounded support in figuring out what's next.
