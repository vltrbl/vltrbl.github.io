Who I am

I come from an international background and hold an international diploma in psychology, after ten years living and working across three countries in Europe. That mix of training and lived experience shows up in how I work — from staffing crisis lines to supporting refugees through unfamiliar medical systems and helping veterans find their footing back in civilian life. What I've learned across all of it: most people don't need to be fixed. They need someone steady to think out loud with.

I offer consultation, not clinical treatment — no jargon, no diagnosis, just a grounded conversation about what's actually going on.
