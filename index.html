Psychological Consultant

Mykyta Melnik

Frankfurt am Main · sessions in English, Ukrainian, and Russian

I'm a psychology graduate (B.Sc., Jacobs University Bremen) with several years of hands-on experience in crisis counselling and psychosocial support. I've worked directly with people navigating anxiety, disrupted sleep, low mood, and difficult life transitions — first as a counsellor at Krisenchat, supporting people through high-risk situations, and more recently as a volunteer counsellor helping veterans adjust to civilian life.

I offer psychological consultation, not clinical treatment: a space to think through what's weighing on you, build coping strategies, and figure out next steps — in whichever of these three languages feels most natural to you.

What I help with

  • Anxiety and persistent worry
  • Sleep difficulties
  • Low mood and depressive episodes (non-clinical support)
  • Relationship and interpersonal difficulties
  • Life transitions and adjustment
